# 🧭 Seattle 4-Day Itinerary (Photography-Focused)

---

# 🌆 DAY 1 — Downtown + Ferry + Skyline (Transit)
**Theme: Iconic Seattle + waterfront + city views**

## Morning
- Pike Place Market  
  - Street photography, vendors, early light

## Midday
- Washington State Ferry → Bainbridge Island  
  - Skyline shots from ferry deck  
  - Walk around Winslow

## Evening
- Sky View Observatory (Columbia Center)  
  - Blue hour + night skyline photography  
  - Best city overview shots

---

# 🌲 DAY 2 — Parks + Forest + Lake (Transit)
**Theme: Green Seattle + calm nature**

## Morning
- Washington Park Arboretum  
  - Forest trails + reflections + Japanese garden

## Midday
- Volunteer Park  
  - Conservatory + water tower views + city framing

## Afternoon
- Seward Park  
  - Lake Washington shoreline + forest loop walk

## Optional Sunset
- Lincoln Park  
  - Driftwood + Puget Sound coastline light

---

# 🚗 DAY 3 — Coastal Nature Loop (Car Day)
**Theme: cliffs + ocean + cinematic landscapes**

## Morning
- Discovery Park  
  - Lighthouse trail + forest + cliff views

## Midday
- Carkeek Park  
  - Beach + train bridge + coastal textures

## Evening
- Ballard Locks (Hiram M. Chittenden Locks)  
  - Boats + water movement + mechanical structures
- Return to:
  - Discovery Park OR Lincoln Park  
  - Golden hour coastline photography

---

# 🌄 DAY 4 — Seattle Deep Cuts (Transit, No Backtracking Loop)
**Theme: skyline + quirky + industrial + water movement**

## Morning
- Kerry Park  
  - Classic Seattle skyline photography

## Late Morning → Midday
- Fremont Troll + Fremont area  
  - Street art + urban textures

## Afternoon
- Gas Works Park  
  - Lake Union skyline + industrial structures  
  - Golden hour reflections

## Optional Finish
- Return downtown  
  - Waterfront or Sky View Observatory (night re-shoot)
